NetScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Summary

NetScope does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data. Everything it shows you stays in memory on your own machine, inside your own browser.

What NetScope processes

When you press “Start capture” in the NetScope side panel, the extension attaches Chrome's own debugging protocol to that one tab and processes its network traffic — HTTP requests and responses (URLs, headers, bodies) and WebSocket frames — for one purpose only: displaying and searching that traffic in the panel, at your request. Chrome shows its standard debugging notice for the whole time capture is active, and capture ends when you press Stop, close the panel, or close the tab.

This processing happens entirely locally, in memory:

  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, telemetry, error reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing is written to disk. Captured traffic is held in memory only while the panel is open and discarded when capture stops (and on page navigation, unless you enable “Preserve log” for that session).
  • Data leaves the extension only when you explicitly ask it to: clicking a Copy button places text on your clipboard, and the AsyncAPI export saves a file through your browser’s normal download mechanism. Both are entirely under your control.

Permissions

  • sidePanel — the user interface lives in Chrome's side panel.
  • debugger — the capture mechanism (the Chrome DevTools Protocol, the same one DevTools uses). Attached to a single tab only after you press Start, visibly (Chrome shows its debugging notice), and detached on Stop or when the panel closes.
  • tabs — reads the active tab's title and URL to label what is being captured.
  • clipboardWrite — used only when you click a Copy button (copy as cURL, copy a frame payload, copy the AsyncAPI document).

NetScope requests no access to website data (no host permissions) and injects no scripts into pages.

Data collection

NetScope collects no personal information, no browsing history, no usage statistics, and no identifiers. There are no accounts, no cookies set by the extension, and no remote code.

Limited Use

NetScope’s handling of any information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Because no data is collected or transferred, there is nothing to sell, share, or use for advertising, creditworthiness, or any other purpose.

Changes
